## Milestone scope

The team went through what needs to be delivered in this milestone:

1. **State of the art:** a more detailed version, with improved related work and the technologies to be used.
2. **Requirements gathering:** define the objectives and collect information through documents, interviews and meetings, including the context in which the system will be used.
3. **Functional requirements:** what the system must do, covering business rules, administrative functions, authentication and authorisation, reports and historical data.
4. **Actors:** a description of the target users.
5. **Use cases:** the interaction between users and the system, organised by packages and supported by a diagram.
6. **Non-functional requirements:** performance, availability, maintainability, security, data integrity, usability and interoperability, along with assumptions and dependencies.
7. **System architecture:** the "skeleton" of the system, designed, implemented and tested. It includes a deployment diagram (hardware, software, middleware and technologies) and, optionally, a domain model.
8. **Mock-ups:** of the interfaces, produced as early as possible.
9. **Presentation:** improved with the feedback the professors gave us.

## Presentation feedback

The team discussed what went less well in the first presentation: the slides were not at the same level as our spoken delivery. They were too weak and carried too much information.

For the next presentation, the slides should be more appealing and easy to read from a distance, instead of dense walls of text or images that can't be read well.
